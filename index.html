Vendor FAQ

A few things you may be wondering.

When are applications accepted?

Applications are accepted while a market is listed as open. Spaces may fill before the final date, so applying early is encouraged.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews each submission for market fit and product mix. Accepted vendors receive next steps by email.

Can I apply for more than one date?

Yes. Select every market or date that works for your business in the relevant application.

How do I secure an accepted spot?

Your acceptance email will include the official payment instructions and deadline.

What if I do not receive an email?

Check your spam and promotions folders, then contact us through the official Made in the 604 website.
